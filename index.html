Small free tools for everyday problems

Each tool here does one ordinary job, like comparing mortgage offers or keeping a daily habit going. They were made with Claude and open on Claude's website, and a real person picked each one.

They're free to use. If one helped you, or got in your way, please tell us. That's how we decide what to keep.

On this page

    The tools

    First time opening one of these?

    1. It's a small app someone made with Claude. The "Open in Claude" button takes you to it on claude.ai, in a new tab.
    2. You'll see the tool inside a Claude page. You don't need to chat with Claude to use it. Just fill in the boxes.
    3. You don't need a Claude account. The tool opens without signing in. The page shows banners inviting you to sign in or get the Claude app; you can ignore them.
    4. Your data stays with you. For the tools listed here, what you type is kept in your own browser on that device. It doesn't move to your other devices, and clearing your browser data erases it.
    5. It's a tool, not advice. The mortgage tools do the arithmetic for you. Check the numbers with your bank or adviser before you decide anything.
    6. Tell us how it went. A two-minute feedback form is at the bottom of this page.
